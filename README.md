# MediaPlayer+

A straightforward media player app for playing audio and video files on your Android device.

[!["Buy Me A Coffee"](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/assets/img/custom_images/orange_img.png)](buymeacoffee.com/zennqiitagp)

## What it does

MediaPlayer Plus lets you open audio and video files stored on your device and play them with a clean, simple interface. It works the way you'd expect a media player to work—nothing fancy, just reliable playback.

1. Play your files. Use the built-in file picker to select one or more audio/video files from your device. You can pick multiple files at once to create a playlist.

2. Keep listening in the background. Switch to another app or turn off the screen, and your music or podcast keeps playing. A notification appears with playback controls so you can pause, skip, or stop without going back to the app.

3. Watch videos in a floating window. On Android 8.0 and later, videos can shrink into a small Picture-in-Picture window when you leave the app. Handy for following along with a video while doing something else.

4. Open files from other apps. When you tap a video or audio file in your file manager, downloads folder, or web browser, Media Player can open it directly. You can also share media files to the app from other apps.

5. Simple controls. Play/pause, skip forward or back 10 seconds, jump to the next or previous track, and drag the progress bar to seek. Tap the screen to show or hide the controls, which fade away automatically during playback.

6. Fullscreen for landscape. Rotate your device to landscape and the video fills the screen, hiding the status bar and navigation buttons for an uninterrupted view.

7. Watch History. The app remembers what you've watched and how far you got. Tap the history icon to see your recently played media, complete with progress bars showing how much you've watched. Tap any item to resume from your last position, or start over if you prefer. Finished items show a checkmark so you can tell at a glance what you've completed. Long-press to remove items you no longer need.

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later

- Picture-in-Picture requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later

## Supported formats

Media Player supports most common audio and video formats, including MP4, MKV, WebM, MP3, AAC, FLAC, and many others. Format support depends on your device's hardware and Android version.
