# MediaPlayer+

A straightforward media player app for playing audio and video files on your Android device.

[!["Buy Me A Coffee"](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/assets/img/custom_images/orange_img.png)](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/zennqiitagp)

## What it does

MediaPlayer Plus lets you open audio and video from your device (and links shared from other apps) with a clean, dark interface built for everyday listening and watching.

1. **Play your files.** Tap **⋮** at the top of the player and choose **Select Media** to pick one or more audio/video files. Multi-select plays them as a queue in the order you chose.
2. **Saved playlists.** From the same menu, **Create Playlist** to save a named list, **Play Playlist** to load one, **Edit Playlist** to rename, reorder, or remove tracks, and **Add Current Track to Playlist** while something is playing. For lists that still work after you restart the app, add tracks using **Select Media** or **Create Playlist** inside MediaPlayer+ when you can—files opened only once from another app may lose access later.
3. **Now playing queue.** Tap the **track title** in the top bar to open the queue: jump to a item, drag to reorder, or remove tracks from what is playing now.
4. **Keep listening in the background.** Switch to another app or turn off the screen, and playback continues. A notification shows play/pause, previous/next, and stop. **Swiping that notification away stops playback.**
5. **Watch videos in a floating window.** On Android 8.0 and later, video can shrink into Picture-in-Picture when you leave the app, with previous, play/pause, and next actions in the PiP window.
6. **Open files from other apps.** Tap audio or video in a file manager, downloads folder, or browser, or **Share** media to MediaPlayer+. HTTP and HTTPS links are supported when another app sends them to the player.
7. **Simple controls.** Play/pause, ±10 seconds, **shuffle**, and **repeat** (off → repeat all → repeat one). Drag the progress bar to seek. For **previous/next track**, swipe left or right on the video area, or use the notification or PiP buttons—the main bar does not use separate skip-track buttons. Tap the screen to show or hide controls; they fade away during playback.
8. **Equalizer & track volume.** **⋮ → Equalizer** opens track volume (remembered between sessions) and, on supported devices, multi-band EQ you can enable, adjust, and reset. Start playback first if bands do not appear; some devices or audio outputs (for example Bluetooth) may not offer an equalizer for this app.
9. **Fullscreen for landscape.** Rotate to landscape and video fills the screen with immersive fullscreen; portrait returns to the normal layout.
10. **Watch history.** The app remembers what you played and how far you got. Tap the history icon for recently played items with progress bars, resume or start over, checkmarks for completed items, and long-press to remove entries. If a file moved or permissions were lost, you get a clear message and can remove the entry from history.
11. **Recents and playback.** Removing MediaPlayer+ from the **Recent apps** list stops playback and closes the session, including while audio or video is actively playing.

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later
- Picture-in-Picture requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later

## Supported formats

MediaPlayer+ supports most common audio and video formats, including MP4, MKV, WebM, MP3, AAC, FLAC, and many others. Format support depends on your device's hardware and Android version. Streaming over **http** or **https** works when you open or share a compatible link from another app.
